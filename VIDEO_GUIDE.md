# FinFlow AI · concept video

**Target duration:** 90 seconds. **Submission:** 6 December 2026.

## 0–15 seconds · The manual handoff

**Narration**

An invoice lands in an inbox. Someone reads the PDF, copies the amount into a spreadsheet, then searches for the purchase order. When the numbers disagree, the next person often has to start that search again.

**On screen**

Show the handoff from inbox to spreadsheet to purchase order.

## 15–30 seconds · FinFlow AI

**Narration**

FinFlow AI is our proposal for the B1 theme, Replace a Department, Not a Task. We are designing one accounts payable workflow that follows each invoice from arrival to a decision the finance team can explain.

**On screen**

Show project title and theme, then the intake, review and handoff stages.

## 30–50 seconds · How an invoice would move

**Narration**

Gemini would read the PDF and propose invoice fields. Explicit rules would verify the vendor, look for duplicates, and match the invoice to the order and goods receipt. If the evidence is missing or amounts disagree, a reviewer would see the reason. A passing invoice could become payment-ready. Finance would still control payment.

**On screen**

Show the planned decision branch and highlight the human review path.

## 50–70 seconds · Inside the repository

**Narration**

Here is our first submission. The routes and controllers reserve a place for web requests. The services folder separates Gemini extraction from financial checks. The architecture document explains how exceptions return to a reviewer. These files are placeholders, so what you see today is the design we intend to build.

**On screen**

Record the real repository file listing and architecture document. Use the local checkout until a GitHub remote is approved and published.

## 70–90 seconds · Roadmap and impact

**Narration**

Next we will connect Express and MongoDB, build invoice and vendor records, then add PDF extraction and matching. Human review, reporting, testing and deployment will follow. We expect fewer manual handoffs and clearer audit decisions. We plan to measure handling time and the share of invoices that need review.

**On screen**

Show the eight phases, then close on the two proposed measures and submission date.

## Recording and editing

Record in landscape at 1280 × 720 or higher. Speak at roughly 150 words per minute and pause between sections. Capture only the scaffold and documentation during the repository segment. After publishing, reshoot that segment with the repository Code tab, then open ARCHITECTURE.md. Hide notifications and private account information. Keep the workflow diagram labeled “Proposed.” Trim or retime each take to the storyboard boundaries, review captions and listen once at normal speed before exporting an H.264 MP4.
